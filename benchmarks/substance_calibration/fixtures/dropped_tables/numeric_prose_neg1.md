# Regional Performance Summary

During the fiscal year, the company reported revenue of $421 million
in North America, up from $387 million in the prior year, representing
a growth rate of 8.8 percent. Europe contributed $312 million,
declining 4.2 percent year over year primarily due to currency
headwinds. The Asia Pacific segment posted its strongest annual result
on record at $214 million, growing 22 percent.

Consolidated gross margin expanded by approximately 240 basis points
to 68.4 percent, driven by a favorable product mix and pricing
discipline in the enterprise software business. Operating margin
reached 21.7 percent for the full year, compared with 19.3 percent
in the prior period.

Free cash flow for the year totaled $187 million, of which $124
million was returned to shareholders through dividends and share
repurchases.
