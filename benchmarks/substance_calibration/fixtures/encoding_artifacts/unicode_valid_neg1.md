# Résumé Guidance

When applying for positions in our international offices, please
ensure your résumé is submitted in PDF format. The following naming
convention should be followed: `LastName_FirstName_Résumé.pdf`.

## Language Requirements

Candidates' documents may be submitted in English or Français, though
English is preferred for the initial screening. Any technical
certifications should include their original credentialing body's
name in native form (e.g., Österreichischer Rundfunk, München
Universität).

## Common Issues

Encoding drift can affect names containing accented characters. If
your résumé displays garbled characters when opened by the reviewer,
please resubmit ensuring the export was performed with UTF-8 encoding
rather than legacy Windows code pages. Naïve conversions from
Latin-1 sources are the most frequent cause.
