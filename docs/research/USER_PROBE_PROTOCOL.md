# One-owner parser-upgrade investigation screen

Status: **prepared, not started**. Research coordinator: **unassigned**.
Participating upgrade owner and team: **unassigned**. No customer evidence has
been collected and no outreach has been conducted for this protocol. A real owner,
authorized artifacts and workflow context are required to start. This document
does not authorize contacting anyone or building a product.

## Decision and scope

Determine whether one recurring, costly investigation step remains after an
owner's competent pytest/diff/source-viewer workflow, and whether one transparent
manual evidence helper merits further study. Existing tools win by default. This
screen can reject a direction; it cannot establish software automation, safety,
semantic preservation, market demand or causal time savings.

Use **one naturally occurring parser/configuration upgrade investigation** with
saved before/after outputs. Cap preparation and observation at **two internal
person-hours and one owner person-hour**, including artifact recovery. Stop when
either budget is exhausted; record incompleteness and any overrun. Do not quietly
exclude preparation or extend the budget to obtain a favorable result.

The protocol operationalizes the local 2026-09-21 product-direction decision. It
is self-contained because that research package is not a published dependency.
No workbench, assertion DSL, semantic engine or benchmark campaign is in scope.

## Before opening the candidate packet

1. Record the owner, team, current task, authorized source and saved outputs,
   parser versions/configurations and available generation records. Freeze source
   and output hashes. Present-byte hashes do not establish historical generation
   identity or correct source-to-output correspondence. A known source mismatch
   invalidates comparison; missing historical binding stays explicitly unresolved.
2. Fix start and end dates for the **preceding 90 days**. Inventory every parser
   or configuration change in that window, including events needing no source
   inspection, unavailable artifacts and unsuccessful investigations. Record
   inspection frequency, actual effort and whether effort is logged or recalled.
   Two selected interesting examples are not a recurrence denominator.
3. Observe the owner's strongest actual baseline: existing fixtures and pytest,
   structured and full/raw diffs, source viewer/page links, hashes, generation
   identities, and supported expected/actual checks. Keep existing tools and
   reasonable assistance equally available. Allow reviewed generic LLM assistance
   only if already used, with equal budgets; no new paid calls are required.
   Existing baseline tests are sunk assets, not new adoption costs. Do not force
   the baseline to implement semantics absent from the helper.
4. Record the costly residual step in the owner's words. Before candidate
   exposure, the owner declares the **minimum absolute person-minutes saved per
   recurring event** that would justify adoption. Select one mechanism before
   inspecting timed outcomes: maintaining known checks, locating/explaining
   changes, or identifying mismatched runs. Stop if no material residual exists.
5. Fix source-based questions and a completion rubric: supported decision,
   required escalation, uncertainties, evidence coverage and remaining work.
   Golden output is not source truth. Where independent adjudication is available,
   give neither operational method privileged access; record evaluation-only
   work separately. If truth cannot be independently established, mark it unknown.

## Prepare and use exactly one helper

Use existing files/tools to produce one packet or check explanation for that
residual step. Label **every field** as existing detector output, mechanically
extracted evidence, or human-authored localization/interpretation. Do not present
a human's diagnosis as current AksharaMD capability or assume future automation.

Retain full/raw diffs and list unknown and unshown regions. For each evidence link,
record source/output byte identity, page and occurrence, origin (owner anchor,
parser metadata, matching, or human inspection), and status (human-confirmed,
ambiguous, or unavailable). Check identity separately from diagnostic quality;
a quality PASS does not validate a link. Recheck anchors when source bytes change.
Unsupported links and ambiguous occurrences remain unresolved.

Have an ordinary maintainer use the packet without its author explaining it.
Record what step actually disappears, unsupported conclusions, expert rescue,
wrong anchors, omissions and unfinished work. Never give the preparer adjudicated
answers while withholding them from the baseline. The same person's second view
of the same case is **qualitative feedback only**, not a causal speed comparison.
A helpful expert packet demonstrates at most possible service/workflow value.

Count all operational person-minutes, by owner/operator/vendor expert: export and
recovery, source authoring, check encoding, localization, construction, integration,
review, corrections, unresolved-case investigation and maintenance. Separate setup,
first-event and repeated-event costs. Any labels/excerpts used in delivery count
as operational work; exclude only independent evaluation-only adjudication/blinding
unavailable to both methods. Report compute costs separately, with no speculative
automation discounts or hidden transfer to scarce expert labor.

## Stop and record the outcome

Stop for a rare/trivial task, a sufficient baseline, no costly residual, invented
owner requirements, a packet that merely repackages the expert's completed
diagnosis, known source mismatch, or exhausted budgets. Misleading reassurance,
missed required escalation and wrong-anchor conclusions block advancement. Record
unsupported closure, false actionable claims, misleading omissions, abstentions,
unresolved cases and remaining work; silence is not correct detection.

No reachable owner is a recruitment limitation. A reachable owner whose current
tools suffice is negative product evidence. An incomplete screen remains
incomplete. A surviving screen permits consideration of the bounded study below,
not product implementation or an immediate claim of adoption.

## Optional later reuse study: prerequisites, not a launched task

Only after the screen survives, obtain a **second independent team** and an
**additional real upgrade from each participating team**. Both owners must do the
actual export/invoke/review/maintain work and choose another real use without
special concierge rescue. Fix mechanism, scope, recipe, baseline and success gates
before examining these cases.

- For each event freeze the complete input inventory and deterministic order;
  take **six consecutive eligible cases** under rules fixed before outcome
  inspection, using the same rule at the second event. Keep all exclusions,
  missing cases and recovery labor visible. Known regressions are supplemental;
  reversed fixes and synthetic mutations are development controls. Neither enters
  routine prevalence or timing denominators.
- Where staffing permits, use separate reviewers for the same case, randomize
  within team/experience blocks, balance order and prohibit same-case repeat
  viewing. Independently adjudicate source-based material changes and acceptable
  decisions before seeing reviewer decisions. Record prior knowledge, withdrawals
  and ambiguous truth. Otherwise stay descriptive with no causal timing claim.
  A second preparer must reproduce the fixed recipe on fresh cases without
  project-expert rescue.
- Cap follow-up at **12 internal preparation/delivery person-hours and eight
  participant person-hours**. If adequate adjudication and comparison cannot fit,
  stop incomplete rather than weaken the evidence.
- Require independently justified decisions at least as good as baseline and
  **zero** observed misleading reassurance, missed required escalation or
  wrong-anchor conclusions. Report false actionable claims/all actionable claims
  and affected documents/all attempted documents; the conservative screen allows
  **at most one affected case per six routine cases**. Raw changes are not defects.
- Retain every attempted case. Require **at least four of six** to materially
  advance an independently justified decision without project-expert rescue.
  Merely telling the user to open the PDF is insufficient. If maintaining checks
  is the selected mechanism, owners author operational facts before candidate
  exposure; log every attempted/rejected requirement and encoding time, with a
  default authoring screen median **at most ten minutes/document**. Do not impose
  an assertion quota on localization work.
- Each team must show **at least 20% lower second-event total person-minutes and
  its predeclared material absolute saving**, plus positive observed two-event
  cumulative savings including incremental setup. Show case differences and team
  totals, also removing the largest-time case. Include maintenance, unresolved
  work and all operational labor. Do not pool away a failing team.

These are prospective investment gates, not established performance or population
risk thresholds. Two teams and six cases do not establish general sensitivity,
safety, average treatment effects or market size. A pass justifies a bounded
component feasibility/conformance experiment; a segment-specific benefit requires
fresh replication. New alignment/semantic science needs its own design and proof.

## Alternative only when an actual consumer has evidence-access pain

Apply the same named-user and recurring-pain entry standard to evidence delivery.
If a real consumer supplies the stronger case, choose that hypothesis **instead
of** the upgrade probe before testing; with equally accessible genuine users,
prefer testing the existing packaging mechanism first. Do not launch both pilots.

Cap this alternative at **four internal hours and one consumer hour**, with three
already diagnosed authorized cases and the existing application. Compare current
packages with the consumer's direct source/page links, attachments or crops;
keep extracted text/structure equal where possible. Require opening the exact
intended assets: local paths, empty checksum placeholders or a successful plan
are not delivery success. Count installation, byte transfer, repair and operator
work. Stop if direct access is equally useful or a new viewer/platform is needed.
Diagnosed cases establish only mechanism usability; prospective routine use and
repeated cost measurement remain necessary. Infer no QA gains or billed savings.
